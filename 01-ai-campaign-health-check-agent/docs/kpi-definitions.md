# KPI Definitions for Campaign Health Check

This file explains the core performance marketing KPIs used in the AI Campaign Health Check Agent.

## 1. Impressions

The number of times an ad was shown.

**Why it matters:**
Impressions show reach and visibility. High impressions with low clicks usually means the audience is seeing the ad but not finding it attractive enough.

## 2. Clicks

The number of times users clicked the ad.

**Why it matters:**
Clicks show interest. However, clicks alone do not mean success. A campaign can have many clicks and still produce poor leads.

## 3. Spend

The amount spent on the campaign, ad group, ad set, or ad.

**Why it matters:**
Spend must be compared with leads, quality, and business outcomes. High spend with low results indicates wasted budget.

## 4. CTR - Click Through Rate

Formula:

```text
CTR = Clicks / Impressions
```

**Why it matters:**
CTR shows how attractive the ad is to the selected audience.

General interpretation:

- Low CTR may indicate weak creative, weak hook, poor offer, wrong audience, or irrelevant keywords.
- High CTR with low conversions may indicate that users are interested but the landing page or offer is weak.

## 5. CPC - Cost Per Click

Formula:

```text
CPC = Spend / Clicks
```

**Why it matters:**
CPC shows how much you are paying for traffic. High CPC is not always bad if conversion quality is strong.

## 6. CPM - Cost Per 1,000 Impressions

Formula:

```text
CPM = Spend / Impressions * 1000
```

**Why it matters:**
CPM shows media cost and audience competition. High CPM can happen in competitive audiences or premium placements.

## 7. Leads

The number of users who completed the desired lead action, such as submitting a form or booking a demo.

**Why it matters:**
Lead volume is useful, but lead quality must also be checked.

## 8. CPL - Cost Per Lead

Formula:

```text
CPL = Spend / Leads
```

**Why it matters:**
CPL shows how much it costs to generate one lead.

Important note:
A low CPL is not always good if leads are poor quality. A higher CPL can be acceptable if leads are sales-qualified.

## 9. CVR - Conversion Rate

Formula:

```text
CVR = Leads / Clicks
```

**Why it matters:**
CVR shows how well traffic converts after clicking the ad.

Common interpretation:

- High CTR + low CVR = landing page, offer, form, or trust issue.
- Low CTR + low CVR = creative/audience mismatch and landing page issue.
- Low CTR + high CVR = niche but strong intent audience.

## 10. Sessions

The number of website visits recorded by analytics.

**Why it matters:**
Sessions help compare ad platform clicks with website visits. If clicks are high but sessions are low, tracking, page load, redirect, or consent issues may exist.

## 11. Engaged Sessions

Sessions where users meaningfully interacted with the site.

**Why it matters:**
Engaged sessions help identify traffic quality.

## 12. Engagement Rate

Formula:

```text
Engagement Rate = Engaged Sessions / Sessions
```

**Why it matters:**
Low engagement rate may indicate poor traffic quality, wrong targeting, slow landing page, weak above-the-fold content, or misleading ad copy.

## 13. Form Starts

The number of users who started filling out the form.

**Why it matters:**
Form starts show stronger intent than clicks.

## 14. Form Submits

The number of users who submitted the form.

**Why it matters:**
Form submits are usually treated as lead conversions.

## 15. Form Completion Rate

Formula:

```text
Form Completion Rate = Form Submits / Form Starts
```

**Why it matters:**
Low form completion rate may indicate form friction.

Possible reasons:

- Too many fields
- Poor mobile experience
- Technical error
- Trust issue
- Unclear value proposition
- Slow page or broken form

## 16. Lead Quality

A business-level indicator showing whether leads are useful for sales.

Example values:

- Good
- Average
- Poor

**Why it matters:**
Marketing should not optimize only for lead volume. It should optimize for quality, sales readiness, and business value.

## Professional Rule

Never judge a campaign using one KPI alone. Always connect:

```text
Ad metric + Website behavior + Conversion data + Lead quality
```

That is how campaign analysis becomes professional.
